Sign in to Stretchy

Before you start (2 minutes)

Download a free authenticator app on your phone: Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator (App Store or Google Play). It adds a second step to signing in, which protects the personal information you’ll see.

How to sign in

1. Tap the button above, or go to stretchyyoga.co.nz/host/login.

2. Use sophie@example.com. It’s the address your profile is linked to. Sign in with a different one and we won’t find you.

3. Tap Continue with Google if it’s a Google or Gmail address. Otherwise type your email and tap Send me a link, then open the link we email you. No password needed.

4. The first time only: you’ll see a QR code. Open your authenticator app, tap + and scan it, then type in the 6-digit code it shows. If you’re signing in on your phone and can’t scan the screen, the page also gives a key you can type into the app instead.

5. Each time after that: sign in the same way, then enter the latest 6-digit code from your app.

New phone, or lost access to your app? Reply to this email and we’ll reset it for you.

Once you’re in

• Your upcoming sessions, and how many people are coming

• The run sheet for each session you’re on: check people in, and see anything they’ve told us about moving with care

• The Team portal: locations, team contacts, and our team agreement. Please read it and tap accept.

• Sessions that need covering, if you’d like to pick one up

Looking after people’s information

The run sheet shows attendees’ first names and any notes they’ve shared about moving with care. That’s personal, so please:

• Use it only to run the session. Don’t share, screenshot or copy it.

• Don’t contact attendees through details you’ve seen on Stretchy.

• Keep your sign-in to yourself, and sign out on shared devices.

• Tell us straight away if anything goes wrong, like a lost phone or a message sent to the wrong person.

Your own name and phone number appear on your team card, so others on your sessions can reach you on the day. You can update them any time in your profile.
