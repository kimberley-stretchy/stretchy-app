Yes, the people dots can be live in every email. When each email is sent, the system reads that session's numbers at that moment and draws the dots: filled for people in, shaded for places still needed to go ahead, a dashed line at the minimum, and empty circles up to full. A 20-person session shows 20 dots, a 30-person session shows 30.

They're built from plain email code (no images), so they work in dark mode and don't need loading. One catch: Outlook on Windows ignores rounded corners, so there the dots show as small squares. Still readable, just less pretty.

Each email is a snapshot from the moment it was sent. The live count is always on the session page.

Checked and consistent across everything:

  • Moments are always called: Up to 36 hrs out (free to cancel) · 36 → 2 hrs out (still room to join, no free cancelling) · 2 hrs out (final price charged, joining closes).
  • Labels use "hrs" (UP TO 36 HRS OUT). Sentences use "hours" ("we'll confirm 36 hours out").
  • "Never more than $X": before 36 hrs, X is always the price at the minimum (e.g. $22.82). After 36 hrs nobody can cancel, so X is the current price.
  • Not enough people → "called off, you pay nothing". We cancel → "you don't pay" (and a full refund if already charged).
  • The pending-amount line appears where a card hold has just been placed (Place held emails) or released (Not this time, Hold cancelled).
  • Buttons: "Hold my place" (book), "Share with a mate" (filled), "Manage my booking" (outline), "Cancel my hold" / "Change or cancel places" (dashed).
  • No "spots", "peeps", "locked in", "better-er", "TBC" or charity lines outside the homepage band.
  • Dates and times all come from each session. The 36 hr and 2 hr moments are described, not dated, on the site and in emails. The exact time only appears in the "you can't cancel now" message.

Part 4

Terms & FAQ wording

The policy itself doesn't change. This is the Terms and FAQ wording rewritten to match the site and emails word for word. Every "Cancellation policy ↗" link lands on this section.

Terms · The short version

Stretchy connects people who want to move with people who teach. You hold a place, the price drops as the room fills, and everyone pays the same final price, charged 2 hours before the session. If a session doesn't reach its minimum by 36 hours before, it's called off and you pay nothing.

Terms · Payment & cancellation (/terms#cancellation)

  1. Hold your place. We save your card and show you the most you'll pay. Nothing is charged yet. Your bank may show a pending amount for a few days. That's a hold, not a payment.
  2. Up to 36 hours before: free to cancel. No charge, no questions.
  3. 36 hours before: going ahead, or not. If we have the minimum number of people, we're on. If not, it's called off and you pay nothing. Free cancelling ends here.
  4. 36 to 2 hours before: still room to join. More people can join and the price keeps dropping for everyone. If you join in this window, it's already going ahead, so there's no free cancelling.
  5. 2 hours before: final price charged. Joining closes and we charge your card the final price. It's never more than the max price shown. You'll get a receipt.
  6. Can't make it after 36 hours? The price stands. We don't refund change-of-mind cancellations after this point, because by then we've committed to your teacher, your GEM and your venue. That's what keeps the pricing fair for everyone who shows up. (This doesn't affect your legal rights. See below.)
  7. If we cancel, you don't pay. If you've already been charged, we refund you in full, back to the card you paid with.
  8. Something serious come up? Illness, an emergency, life. Get in touch and we'll look at it case by case.

FAQ answers

When do I get charged? 2 hours before the session, at the final price. When you hold your place we save your card, and your bank may show a pending amount. That's a hold, not a payment.

What if I need to cancel? Free up to 36 hours before. After that, the price stands. Something serious come up? Get in touch.

Can I still join after 36 hours? Yes, up to 2 hours before, if there's room. It's already going ahead, so there's no free cancelling once you join.

What if a session doesn't reach its minimum? It's called off at 36 hours before and you pay nothing.

Why is there a pending amount on my bank? It's a temporary hold for the most you'll pay. It isn't a payment and it drops off on its own. You're only charged the final price, 2 hours before.

I'm holding more than one place. How does that work? Everything works the same, per place. You can change or cancel places for free up to 36 hours before.

Still worth a lawyer's eye before more people book: point 6 (no refund after 36 hours) and the Consumer Guarantees Act wording in "Your rights".
