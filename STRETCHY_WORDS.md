# Stretchy word list (customer-facing copy)

Use these words on every screen, email, push notification and error message. Final version agreed with Kimberley, 12 Oct 2026. Final charge moved from 2 hrs to 30 mins out, 18 Nov 2026.

## The three moments
- **Up to 36 hrs out:** Free to cancel. "If we get {min} people, we're on. Not enough people? You pay nothing."
- **36 hrs → 30 mins out:** Still room to join. "More people can join and the price keeps dropping. No free cancelling from here."
- **30 mins out:** Final price charged. Joining closes. "Never more than $X."

## Rules
- **Keep it short.** One idea per line. No line repeats another nearby.
- Labels use "HRS" / "MINS" (UP TO 36 HRS OUT, 30 MINS OUT). Sentences use "hours" / "minutes" ("we'll confirm 36 hours out", "charged 30 minutes before").
- Before 36 hrs out, "never more than $X" uses the price at the minimum. After 36 hrs, the current price.
- Session pages and emails describe the 36 hr / 30 min moments without dates. The exact time only appears in the "you can't cancel now" message.
- Every session detail (times, venue, teacher, GEM, bring list, numbers, prices) is pulled from the session. Hide empty rows, never "TBC".
- "How it works" (`/#how-it-works`) and "Cancellation policy" (`/terms#cancellation`) always open in a new tab with ↗.
- When in doubt about policy, link to `/terms#cancellation`. Don't restate the long reasoning.
- Brand: BN Chubb (display), Space Grotesk (body), JetBrains Mono (labels) on the site. Emails keep the existing `lib/stretchy-email.ts` system.
