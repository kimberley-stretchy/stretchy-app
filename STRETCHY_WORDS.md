# Stretchy word list (customer-facing copy)

Use these words on every screen, email, push notification and error message. Final version agreed with Kimberley, 12 Oct 2026.

## The three moments
- **Up to 36 hrs out:** Free to cancel. "If we get {min} people, we're on. Not enough people? You pay nothing."
- **36 → 2 hrs out:** Still room to join. "More people can join and the price keeps dropping. No free cancelling from here."
- **2 hrs out:** Final price charged. Joining closes. "Never more than $X."

## Say / don't say
| Moment / thing | Say | Don't say |
|---|---|---|
| Booking button | **Hold my place** | "Hold my place — no charge yet", "$0 today" |
| What you hold | **your place** / **your {n} places** | spot, peeps |
| Up to 36 hrs out | **Free to cancel** | cancellation window, lock-in |
| 36 hrs out | **going ahead** / **called off** · "we're on" / "it's on" | locked in, minimum viable mats |
| 36 → 2 hrs out | **Still room to join** · price **keeps dropping** · **No free cancelling from here** | better-er |
| 2 hrs out | **Final price charged** · joining closes | locked & loaded, locked in, final price paid |
| Top price | **$X max per person** · **never more than $X** | ceiling, current starting price, never more than you saw |
| Card | **We save your card** · "That's a hold, not a payment." | card authorisation, committed |
| We cancel | **If we ever cancel, you don't pay.** (+ full refund if already charged) | "refunded in full, always" when nothing was charged |
| Headcount | **people** · "10 more to go ahead" · "17 in · still room to join" · "30 max" | peeps, spots to minimum |
| Arriving | **Say kia ora** · meet your teacher, then your GEM | — |
| Emails | "Hey {name}," · sign-off "See you there, The Stretchy team" | — |
| Charity | **Cancer Support NZ**, homepage band only | charity lines anywhere else |

## Rules
- Labels use "HRS" (UP TO 36 HRS OUT). Sentences use "hours" ("we'll confirm 36 hours out").
- Before 36 hrs out, "never more than $X" uses the price at the minimum. After 36 hrs, the current price.
- Session pages and emails describe the 36 hr / 2 hr moments without dates. The exact time only appears in the "you can't cancel now" message.
- Every session detail (times, venue, teacher, GEM, bring list, numbers, prices) is pulled from the session. Hide empty rows, never "TBC".
- "How it works" (`/#how-it-works`) and "Cancellation policy" (`/terms#cancellation`) always open in a new tab with ↗.
- When in doubt about policy, link to `/terms#cancellation`. Don't restate the long reasoning.
- Brand: BN Chubb (display), Space Grotesk (body), JetBrains Mono (labels) on the site. Emails keep the existing `lib/stretchy-email.ts` system.
